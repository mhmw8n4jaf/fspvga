Explanation

An itchy scalp can sometimes be linked to hair loss. This connection often arises from conditions that cause irritation or inflammation of the scalp, which can damage hair follicles.

Possible Causes:

Sensitive Scalp: Some people have a sensitive scalp, which can lead to itching and discomfort. This sensitivity may contribute to hair loss if the scalp is frequently irritated.

Inflammation: Conditions that cause scalp inflammation, such as certain skin disorders, can lead to both itching and hair loss.
