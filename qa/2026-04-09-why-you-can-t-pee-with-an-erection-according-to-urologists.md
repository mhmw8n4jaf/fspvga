Signs You Could Have a Medical Problem

Struggling to pee with an erection isn’t typically a medical problem. But if you’re having trouble urinating when you don’t have an erection, you should see your doctor, Dr. Babbar says.

For younger guys, it could be an anatomical problem, such as a narrowing of the urethra, or a kidney or bladder dysfunction, he explains. For men in their 50s and older, trouble urinating can be a sign of an enlarged prostate.

“If we can intervene, we can help a lot of those patients,” Dr. Babbar says.
