Bloody mucus from the nose and throat can be unsettling. There are many reasons why this might happen, ranging from mild conditions to more serious ones. Common causes of bloody mucus include: • Upper respiratory infections like the flu or the common cold • Dry nasal passages and harsh nose blowing • Chronic sinusitis • Allergies or environmental irritants • Physical trauma to the nose or throat • Prolonged or forceful coughing These most common causes usually don't require immediate medical attention, but it's important to be aware of warning signs that could indicate something more serious like pneumonia or, in rare cases, lung cancer. For instance, if the bloody mucus is accompanied by difficulty breathing, chest pain, or unexplained weight loss, you should seek medical evaluation right away.
