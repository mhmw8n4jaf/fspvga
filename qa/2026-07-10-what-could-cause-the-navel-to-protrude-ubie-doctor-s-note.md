When you notice your belly button sticking out more than usual, there are a few possible reasons. One common cause is an umbilical hernia. This happens when a small hole or weak spot in the muscles around the navel allows internal tissues or fat to push out. It is something that can occur in both children and adults. Another reason could be fluid buildup in the abdomen, known as ascites, which is common in conditions like liver cirrhosis; the extra fluid can push the navel forward. Additionally, if there is a problem with one side of the abdominal wall, such as a weakening or muscle imbalance, it might cause the navel to shift or appear more prominent. Sometimes changes in weight or scarring from previous surgeries can also alter the normal appearance of the belly button. If you notice a persistent change and it worries you, it’s a good idea to get it checked by a doctor, who can perform an exam and decide if any treatment is necessary.
