Upper right abdominal pain commonly originates from the liver or gallbladder. While it may be mild and resolve on its own, you should be concerned if the pain becomes severe, persists, or intensifies rapidly. If you develop additional symptoms like fever, chills, vomiting, or yellowing of the skin and eyes (signs of liver or bile problems), seek immediate medical attention. Pay attention to your body's signals. If something feels wrong or the pain becomes unbearable, don't hesitate to act. Getting medical help promptly can lead to early diagnosis and appropriate treatment.
