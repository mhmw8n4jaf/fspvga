# Who’s Still Together From ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 5?

Reality TV shows often advertise their upcoming seasons as the most dramatic yet, but Love Is Blind season 5 really meant it. This season has featured more love triangles, breakups, and shake ups than any other season, and for the first time ever, we only had two weddings during the finale, and people (rightfully) dropped out during every part of the experiment, from the pods to the couples vacation in Mexico.

Unfortunately, all of this drama and messiness hasn't really translated to fans, as many people have spoken about how they miss the love and commitment shown on previous seasons, especially season 4. The good news is we still have a reunion to straighten things out, and while Netflix hasn’t officially renewed the series yet, there could be more to come.

But back to season five. The last time we saw the couples, Stacy and Izzy and Lydia and Milton were applying for their marriage licenses and getting ready to head down the aisle, but both couples had their own issues to work through before making things official and saying I do.

Since this is Love is Blind, anything can happen, and we have information on what happened during the show's finale, as well as where the couples are now. Since the show was filmed in spring 2022, you may be surprised to hear that some of the cast’s relationship statuses have changed.Here's what you need to know about the Love is Blind season five couples now.

Taylor & JP

Taylor and JP were the first couple to get engaged in the pods, and they didn't have any major issues until they met up in person. Their reveal was awkward, but most fans chalked it up to JP just being nervous. However, during the couples getaway, they barely spoke to each other, even though Taylor tried to engage JP in conversation multiple times. She decided to try one last time to ask him what had changed, and he said that he felt differently about her because he didn't like the fact that she was wearing makeup during the reveal.

Taylor was understandably incredulous at this confession, and JP's further comments about her appearance didn't really make sense. She decided to sleep in a separate hotel room that night, and the next day she broke things off of JP and returned his ring.

In a confessional at the end of the season, JP admitted that his reluctance to open up had cost him many relationships in the past. The two are not together now.

At the reunion, JP apologized and realized how hurtful his comments were, while Taylor posits JP was never attracted to her after they met in person. As for their current relationship status, JP said he had been with someone new for about a year now, and Taylor also said that she was seeing someone.
