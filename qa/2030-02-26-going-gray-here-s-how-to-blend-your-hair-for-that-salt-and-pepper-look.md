Going gray is a journey. Whether you start seeing sprinkles of silver coming in at age 20 or 40, it's often a slow transition from whatever your natural hair color is to full-on silver fox. While it’s associated with “getting old,” the salt and pepper hair look is always in style. Some of Hollywood's biggest stars (notably the great George Clooney) have made the process of going gray one that many men actually look forward to. I mean, who doesn't want to look like George Clooney?

Men who remain a little apprehensive about this transition might be tempted to dye their hair in an effort to stave off going gray. But dyeing your hair every few weeks is just delaying the inevitable and costing you hundreds of hours in the salon chair and thousands of dollars and every year. Sooner or later, everyone goes gray.
