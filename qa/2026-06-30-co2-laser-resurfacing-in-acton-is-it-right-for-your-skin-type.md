What Is CO2 Laser Resurfacing?

CO2 laser resurfacing is a non-invasive skin treatment that uses carbon dioxide (CO2) laser technology to remove damaged layers of skin. The laser works by creating tiny columns of heat to stimulate collagen production while removing the outer layer of damaged skin cells. This results in smoother, firmer skin and reduces imperfections like wrinkles, acne scars, sun damage, and more.
