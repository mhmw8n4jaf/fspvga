# Want to Level Up Leg Day? All You Need Is Your Body Weight and This Workout.

LOWER BODY DAY at the gym usually means cycling between several different stations or machines and waiting patiently for your chance to grab a few free weights or a squat platform. Before you know it, you’ve spent an hour to barely finish four exercises.

To combat that, David Pegram, NASM-CPT, built this ultra-efficient workout that clocks in at around 25 minutes and packs all the punch of the gym without single piece of gear.

[Is Your](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/)

To start, you’ll get a muscle-activating warmup with a couple rounds of world’s greatest stretch and squat shifts. Then, you’ll blast your quads, hamstrings, and glutes with a mix of squats, single-leg RDLs, forward lunges, and single-leg glute bridges. Give it a try and leg day will never be the same.

The Workout

Warmup

World’s Greatest Stretch: 2 reps per side

[All You Need](https://cellular.wiki/1de0ip43n6/)

Squat Shift: 4 reps per side

Workout

[Leg Day All](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/8157616348.html)

Bodyweight Squat: 3 sets 15 of reps

Single-Leg RDL: 4 sets of 10 reps per side

Forward Lunge: 3 sets of 20 reps

Single-Leg Glute Bridge: 3 sets of 6 reps per side

Make It Your Own

If you’re having trouble balancing on one foot during your single-leg RDLs, hold on to a chair or wall for stability. And feel free to adjust your non-working leg placement on single-leg glute bridges. Whether you extend it straight or bend it at 90 degrees, you’ll effectively target your glutes.
