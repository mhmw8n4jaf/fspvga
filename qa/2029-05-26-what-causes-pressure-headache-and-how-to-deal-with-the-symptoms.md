What is a Pressure Headache?

Pressure headaches are often described as tightness and pressure around your forehead and in the back of your head and neck area. It is often accompanied by dull pain. Others would describe the feeling of having a clamp around the skull.

Pressure headaches are also called stress headaches. The main difference between pressure headaches and migraines is that pressure headaches won’t affect your daily activities because it doesn’t affect your vision or balance.
