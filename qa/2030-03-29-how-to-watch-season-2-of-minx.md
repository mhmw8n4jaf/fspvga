Minx is officially back for a second season of gender politics, '70s fashion, and smut. The unconventional workplace comedy stars British actress Ophelia Lovibond as Joyce Prigger, a feminist activist who struggles to find a platform in the chauvinist Los Angeles of the '70s. That is, until she joins forces with Doug Renetti, a pornography publisher played by Spider-Verse star Jake Johnson. Together they create Minx, the first erotic magazine geared towards a female audience.

Inspired by the story of real-life magazines like Playgirl and Viva, Minx premiered on HBO in March 2022, and quickly garnered fans thanks to richly layered performances from its ensemble cast—and some suitably steamy on-screen nudity.

Season 1 ended with Johnson and Lovibond's characters parting ways, leaving the fate of the titular magazine and its employees in question. In a case of life imitating art, the future of the show then also became threatened: while it was initially renewed for a second season on HBO's streaming service Max, that decision was reversed and the show got canceled.

Fortunately, the series had already acquired such a passionate audience that its cancelation led to several other streaming services vying for the rights, meaning that Minx was swiftly able to find a new home, and Season 2 was back on.
