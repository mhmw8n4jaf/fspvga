When you experience pain in your stomach and back along with diarrhea, there are several things that might be happening.

One common cause is gastroenteritis. This is an infection in your stomach and intestines caused by a virus or bacteria. Along with stomach pain and diarrhea, you might also feel pain that spreads to your back.

Another reason could be pancreatitis. This is when your pancreas, an organ near your stomach, becomes inflamed and causes pain that often radiates to the back. Sometimes, infections that do not start in the gut can also cause diarrhea along with body aches, including back pain. These infections can make your whole body hurt as it fights the illness.

Dehydration from diarrhea may add to muscle cramps and worsen back pain. Since these symptoms can be a sign of different issues, it is important to check your symptoms with Ubie's free AI-powered symptom checker or speak with a healthcare provider. They can ask questions, do tests, and help figure out what is causing your pain so you can get the proper treatment and feel better.
