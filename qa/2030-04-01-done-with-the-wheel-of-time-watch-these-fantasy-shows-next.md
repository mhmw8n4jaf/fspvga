The Shannara Chronicles (2016 - 2017)

An adaptation of the fantasy novels by Terry Brooks, the series follows a trio as they encounter adventures and war while trying to protect an ancient tree that is the boundary between their world and the world of evil demons. Merlin (2008 - 2012)

As you can tell by the name, this pick is loosely based on the legends of Merlin and King Arthur. A young Merlin is sent to Camelot to protect Prince Arthur with his magical gifts, but he has to hide his powers because magic is banned in Camelot. The British series follows how their bond evolves over time.

Ragnarok (2020 - 2023)

With three six-episode seasons, you can binge this one in one weekend. The Norwegian fantasy drama follows Magne, a teenage boy that learns he’s a reincarnation of Thor, as he fights against a family of supernatural beings that are hiding their true forms as they pollute the environment. Other Norwegian gods show up as the series progresses.
