What are the side effects of Tretinoin Active Renewal Cream?

• Sun sensitivity (always wear SPF 30+ when using this product).

• Mild irritation can occur, including redness, dryness, peeling and/or flaking.

Can I pair my other skincare products with Tretinoin Active Renewal Cream?

• It’s safe to use the following products: Estriol Cream, hyaluronic-acid serums, peptides, ceramide moisturizers, azelaic acid, mineral sunscreen.

• Skin irritation may occur when using the following products: Leave-on glycolic or salicylic acids, alcohol toners, abrasive scrubs, benzoyl peroxide, or other leave-on retinoids.

• Ask your clinician for guidance if you have any concerns.

What should I know about safety?

• This product is not recommended during pregnancy.

• Use with caution if breastfeeding and avoid chest area.

• Always wear sunscreen when using tretinoin.

• Avoid combining with other exfoliants or retinoids unless directed by your clinician.
