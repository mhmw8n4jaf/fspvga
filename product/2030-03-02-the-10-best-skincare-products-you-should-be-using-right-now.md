Skincare is more than what soap you’re using to wash your face. Though, let’s start there. You shouldn’t use the same soap you wash your body to wash your face. There is a reason skincare products are made for your face. Facial skin, while technically part of the same organ, requires a little more care. It’s more sensitive and can have its own set of issues, from acne to wrinkles to rosacea.

Skincare products can address any of the issues you hope to prevent or control much better than your bar soap. But not all are created equal. Whether you’re hoping to have less wrinkles, zap zits, cut through excess oil, or just generally look fresher and more alert, there’s a skincare product that can help you. Start simple, with a good cleanser and moisturizer, and work your way up from there. As for what to use?
