What are the health implications of having a BMI of 28?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A BMI of 28 falls within the overweight range (25–29.9) and may raise your risk of health conditions such as heart disease, type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, and sleep apnea. While BMI is a useful screening tool, it doesn't account for muscle mass, body composition, or other individual health factors—so it's only part of the picture.
