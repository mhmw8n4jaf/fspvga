What are the initial treatments for Lyme disease?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lyme disease is typically treated with a course of oral antibiotics, most commonly doxycycline, amoxicillin, or cefuroxime axetil. When started in the early stages of infection, these medications are highly effective and usually resolve symptoms within a few weeks. Treatment choice depends on your age, medical history, allergies, and whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

Because Lyme disease symptoms — such as fatigue, joint pain, rash, or fever — can mimic many other conditions, getting an accurate picture of what's happening in your body early is critical. The sooner Lyme is identified, the more effective antibiotic treatment tends to be.
