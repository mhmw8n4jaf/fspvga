What Else Can We Help You With?

What kinds of care does Midi provide?

Midi offers specialized treatment for the common symptoms of perimenopause and menopause, as well as preventative care to help you strengthen your long-term health. Explore specific solutions by exploring our "Care Needs" section in the navigation bar.

What should I expect on my first visit with a Midi clinician?

Your Midi clinician will use this time to get to know you, your symptoms, your full health history, and your personal health goals. These virtual visits are about 30 minutes.

Can I schedule with a specific clinician?

If you would like to see one clinician in particular, go to the Meet Our Clinicians page and search for the clinician you would like to schedule with. Found the clinician you'd like to see? Click the "Book a Visit With Me" button.

How did you find that Midi patients report an average weight loss of 19lbs after at least 6 months of care?

This was based on an internal survey and analysis of 4,991 patients' self-reported weights at least 6 months from their initial Midi weight loss consultation. Patients used a variety of treatments, diet, and exercise. Individual results may vary.
