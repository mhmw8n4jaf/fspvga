What to Look For in Camel Coats

Color: This one is obvious, but you still want to stick to lighter khakis that match with just about everything. Avoid darker brown shades of camel because they are harder to style.

Fabric: Camel coats are primarily built for fall and winter, so you want a warmer, heartier fabric. Look for wool and wool blends that will lock in your body heat and keep out the elements. If you have a bigger budget to work with, look for cashmere and alpaca blends that are softer and more luxurious.

Length: Longer cut men’s coats are in fashion right now, and that is a welcome trend for the winter. Keeping your knees covered will do wonders for keeping your whole body warm. On the other hand, a shorter cut jacket will be more versatile to style and accommodate a wider range of temperatures.

Pockets and Lapels: Camel coats traditionally fall into the topcoat category, which means that they should have suit jacket-type features. Look for a notch or peak lapel, two hand pockets, and a single breast pocket. The hand pockets are often much larger than suit pockets, which you want for a coat.

How to Wear Camel Coats

Camel coats fall into the topcoat category, so they are on the formal side. You can wear them over suits and formalwear, which was the original design. However, camel coats look great with casual clothing as well. Mix it up by wearing one with a hoodie and joggers. You can even use it as your go-to everyday jacket.

The camel color is great because it matches just about everything, that is why the style of jacket is so popular. Navy and denim blues complement camel especially well. So a camel coats always look great with jeans, navy chinos, or a navy suit.
