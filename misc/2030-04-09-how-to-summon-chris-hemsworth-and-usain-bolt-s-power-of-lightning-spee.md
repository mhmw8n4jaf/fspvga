# How To Summon Chris Hemsworth and Usain Bolt's Power of Lightning Speed

The god of thunder and a bolt of lightning came together this week at the Commonwealth Games in Australia—and if you're dreaming of a new, super-speedy member of the Avengers joining up to fight Josh Brolin's Thanos in the upcoming Infinity War, well, consider your wish granted.

Aussie Chris Hemsworth was on his home turf taking in the track and field events at the Games where he was joined by none other than real-life superhero and fastest man on Earth Usain Bolt. Hemsworth jokingly wrote that he was recruiting for Marvel's super squad, throwing some shade at the DC Universe's Justice League member The Flash for good measure.

Bolt wasn't competing in any events, since he retired from competition last year, so Hemsworth didn't have the chance to see lightning hit the track. Instead, the two relaxed in the stands, giving fans a chance to just imagine how Bolt could fit into the Avengers universe. Who would win in a race: Bolt, or Thor?

Bolt might not be hitting the track again anytime soon—and no, he won't actually be joining the Avengers as much as we might wish—but that doesn't mean that you can't add some sprint training to your workout routine to emulate the world's fastest man.

Here are two workouts: one for basic sprint work you can perform just about anywhere, and another that simulates race prep drills runners use to build up their speed.

You can perform this seven round routine anywhere you have room to run—on the road, a treadmill, a track, or even your backyard. All you need is a timer and open space.

Sprint hard for 20 seconds, then jog for 60 seconds. Repeat for seven rounds. Rest for 4 minutes.
