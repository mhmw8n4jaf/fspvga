For higher concentrations of B12, look for leaner cuts of beef. To preserve the vitamin B12 content, avoid frying it and instead opt for prepping it on the grill. While it is not as high in concentration, it more than makes up for it with the presence of the entire gamut of B vitamins.

Oral supplements

Vitamin B12 in the form of pills you swallow has been around for decades. While it is cost-effective and easily accessible for most people, they are historically difficult for the body to absorb and assimilate. The reason for this is that most supplements contain a synthetic form of B12, Cyanocobalamin, that is not ready to be used in its present form.

In order for Cyanocobalamin to be converted, it must be processed using something called intrinsic factor, which is produced in the stomach. The problem is that your ability to assimilate this form of B12 will be limited by how many intrinsic factors your body is able to produce.
