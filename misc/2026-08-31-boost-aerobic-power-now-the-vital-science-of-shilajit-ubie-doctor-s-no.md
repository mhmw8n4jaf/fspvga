Aerobic power—often referred to as cardio endurance—is your body's ability to deliver and use oxygen during sustained exercise. Whether you're training for a 5K, a triathlon, or simply aiming for better daily stamina, understanding how to boost aerobic power is key. In recent years, traditional herbal supplements like shilajit have drawn attention for their potential to support endurance. Below, we'll explore the science behind aerobic power, examine what shilajit is, and review credible evidence on "Shilajit and cardio endurance."

What Is Aerobic Power?

Aerobic power reflects how efficiently your heart, lungs, blood vessels and muscles work together to sustain exercise. Key concepts include:

VO₂ max: the maximum volume of oxygen you can consume per minute, per kilogram of body weight. Higher VO₂ max equals better endurance.

Mitochondrial density: more mitochondria in muscle cells means more energy (ATP) produced.

Cardiovascular adaptations: over time, endurance training enlarges heart chambers, increases stroke volume (blood pumped per beat) and improves capillary networks.

Oxygen delivery: haemoglobin in red blood cells carries oxygen from lungs to muscles.

Improving aerobic power typically involves consistent cardiovascular training (running, cycling, swimming) plus adequate rest, nutrition and—potentially—targeted supplements.

How Shilajit Might Support Cardio Endurance

Research into "Shilajit and cardio endurance" is still emerging, but several mechanisms have been proposed:

Enhanced Mitochondrial Function

Animal and in vitro studies suggest fulvic acid and dibenzopyrones in shilajit may stimulate mitochondrial biogenesis (creation of new mitochondria).

More mitochondria can translate to higher ATP production, fueling muscle contractions during prolonged exercise.

Antioxidant and Anti-Inflammatory Effects

Intense training produces free radicals and inflammation, which can cause fatigue and muscle damage.

Shilajit's antioxidants may neutralize excess free radicals, potentially reducing fatigue and speeding recovery.

Improved Oxygen Utilization

Some research indicates that shilajit supplementation can raise hemoglobin levels, enhancing oxygen transport to muscles.

Better oxygen delivery supports higher VO₂ max and delays the onset of exhaustion.

Electrolyte and Mineral Support

Trace minerals in shilajit—magnesium, potassium, iron—play roles in muscle contraction, nerve function and energy production.

Adequate mineral status is critical for maintaining performance and preventing cramps.

Key Studies to Note

While human trials are limited, here are a few relevant findings:

A double-blind trial (40 healthy volunteers) noted a significant rise in testosterone and a subjective improvement in strength and stamina after shilajit supplementation for 8 weeks.

Rodent studies report increased endurance capacity on treadmill tests following shilajit administration, alongside markers of improved mitochondrial function.

Pilot research hints at reduced oxidative stress markers in athletes taking shilajit, potentially aiding faster recovery.

Limitations and Caveats

Human sample sizes are often small. Larger, well-controlled trials are needed to confirm benefits.

Product quality varies. Some market samples are contaminated with heavy metals or lack active fulvic compounds.

Individual responses differ. Genetics, diet, training status and overall health influence outcomes.

Integrating Shilajit into a Comprehensive Endurance Plan

Shilajit is not a magic bullet. For lasting gains in cardio endurance, focus on:

Structured training: a mix of steady-state cardio, intervals and cross-training.

Progressive overload: gradually increasing workout intensity or duration.

Recovery strategies: sleep, stretching, foam rolling and active recovery days.

Balanced nutrition: carbs for fuel, protein for repair, healthy fats and micronutrients.

Stress management: meditation or breathing exercises to keep cortisol in check.

Shilajit can be one piece of the puzzle—potentially enhancing energy metabolism and cutting down recovery time when used responsibly.

Final Thoughts

Research into "Shilajit and cardio endurance" offers promising leads, especially around mitochondrial health and antioxidant support. Yet, more robust human studies are needed to confirm optimal dosing and long-term effects. If you choose to try shilajit:
