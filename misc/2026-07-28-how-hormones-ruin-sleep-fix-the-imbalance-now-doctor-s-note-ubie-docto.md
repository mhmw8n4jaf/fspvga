Explanation

Hormonal changes in the body can have a significant impact on sleep. During the menstrual cycle, hormones like estrogen and progesterone fluctuate, which can affect sleep quality and duration. Some women may find it harder to sleep well right before their period starts.

In perimenopause, the time before menopause, hormonal levels change a lot, which can also affect sleep. Some women may experience night sweats, which can wake them up at night. Others might have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep.
