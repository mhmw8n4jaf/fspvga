Understanding Diabetes Mellitus

There are three main types of diabetes:

Type 1 diabetes is usually genetic. This type of diabetes is unrelated to lifestyle factors and is usually diagnosed early in life.

Type 2 diabetes is typically diagnosed later on in life and is typically caused by poor dietary choices.

Gestational diabetes is a temporary form of diabetes that occurs during pregnancy. This type goes away once the pregnancy is complete.
