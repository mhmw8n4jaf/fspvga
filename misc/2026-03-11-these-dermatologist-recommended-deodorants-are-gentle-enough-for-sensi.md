How Should People With Sensitive Skin Apply Deodorant?

Apply two or three strokes to clean dry skin. “For a true deodorant, you can apply it in the morning or before sweating, since the goal is to help prevent odor,” McTighe says. “For antiperspirant, apply a few strokes to the dry skin of each underarm before bedtime and allow it to dry. Since sweating is minimal overnight, this gives the antiperspirant more time to be absorbed and work effectively. Avoid applying deodorant or antiperspirant to irritated, broken skin or immediately after shaving.”

If you’re not sure if your new deodorant or antiperspirant will cause a reaction, try a patch test first: Apply a small amount of product to the back of your forearm and wait at least 24 hours, if possible. The American Academy of Dermatology recommends even more rigorous patch testing, by applying a small amount of the product to a test spot twice daily for 7 to 10 days.
