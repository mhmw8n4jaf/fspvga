What is vitamin D for?

Vitamin D is one of the essential nutrients in our body. It is responsible for producing calcium and phosphorous, two of the necessary building blocks of bones. Studies also show that vitamin D slows cancer cell growth and helps reduce inflammation and infection in the joints and other parts of the body.
