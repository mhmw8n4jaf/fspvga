He was diagnosed with this brutal, relentless form of malignant cancer only three weeks ago. Diamond first played Screech in the sitcom Good Morning, Miss Bliss, which only aired for one season in 1988-1989 before being re-tooled into Saved By The Bell. Diamond was able to transition the character into the new show, which he appeared on for 86 episodes before also appearing in spinoff series Saved By The Bell: The College Years and Saved By The Bell: The New Class.

Diamond had difficulty after Saved By The Bell ended, finding himself typecast due to his popular role. “I didn’t know what to do [when the show ended],” he said in 2013. “It was hard to get work that wasn’t Screech-clone stuff.”

He also later said that the book was ghostwritten, and many of the stories were untrue.
