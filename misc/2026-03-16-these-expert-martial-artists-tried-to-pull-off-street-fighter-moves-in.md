# These Expert Martial Artists Tried to Pull Off 'Street Fighter' Moves in Real Life

Seven-time karate champion and action movie stuntwoman Gemma Nguyen along with her counterpart, martial artist Noah Fleder, are recreating some of the most popular moves from 'Street Fighter' for their YouTube channel Gamology. In a recent video, the duo took to the mat to try out 16 moves from characters including Cammy, Ryu, Chun-Li, Ken, Karin, Zangief, and F.A.N.G.

Move #1: CammyIn this move, Cammy does a 720-degree spin (two spins in the air) into a back fist. Nguyen is confident she can do this, and give it a shot.

"The hardest part about this move was basically learning to spot and be aware of where you are when you're spinning," she says. "And the back fist was actually more challenging than I thought it would be because you have to open up prior to be able to stick that back fist because you're basically cutting your rotation cold turkey." And to be able to land it in that front stance pose that she does is pretty difficult.

Move #1: Ryu

This move is actually two strikes with one kick. "Dude just killed 2 birds with 1 stone," says Nguyen. "He goes back leg axe kick, hits him on the way up, and then boom hits him on the way down again. Combo!" Felder tries this one out first. Nguyen notes that in real life this one might not work.

"It works with paddles because he's able to kick through the paddle, but say for example if it's something that's going to stop your kick, it's going to be hard to continue the momentum going up to execute the second kick," says Nguyen.

Move #1: Chun-Li

This move is 4 kicks, all front to side kicks."Notice the back leg is pivoted which means my heel should be facing my target, and between every kick you're getting that re-chamber," says Nguyen.

Move #1: Ken

This move is a double knee. "He does one-knee, two-knee into a back leg front kick, and homeboy goes flying,"says Nguyen. Felder takes this one. "His foot is not touching the ground, he's just kind of taping to make sure his knee stays up high. He's going knee-tap-knee into a front kick," says Nguyen.

Move #1: Karin

"She wants to see your manager!" says Nguyen. "She grabs his hand, goes underneath, and throws him. She makes it look so easy. This one is all dependent on the person taking the fall, so whether or not they can sell it is important here. It's all up to Noah to really sell that throw."

Move #1: Zangief

"He goes in for a straight bear hug, straight man-handles this fool, grabs him from behind the back and underneath the leg, lifts him up and gives him a nice throw," says Nguyen.

Move #2: Chun-Li

This move is a nice strong side kick. "Every kick has 3 parts: the chamber, the kick (long extension, stick it), and then recoil and down," says Nguyen.

Move #1: F.A.N.G

This move involves using your knees to hit the back of your opponents knees, followed by an axe kick. "This was really awkward trying to get this to land. I've never had someone strike me with their knees, that was an interesting design element into an axe kick," says Nguyen.

Move #2: Cammy

"This one was actually a really scary move for both of us," says Nguyen. "It basically requires me getting onto Noah's shoulders, faking the neck snap, and the scariest part going off of his back. He had to hold my legs in order for me to fully extend backwards and reach for the ground. That was super scary and that was my first time trying a move like that."

Move #2: Ryu

"It looks like he's doing a 720-degree kick, but the leg is extended the hole time. That's gonna be really difficult to do in real life unless you've got the height for it," says Nguyen.

So to re-create Felder attempted this one and did a 'cheat 720', which is a "chamber, chambering again, and throwing the third leg out for a spinning hook kick."

Move #2: Karin

"Karin grabs the guy, deflects his hand, blocks his other hand and goes in and hit shim with her shoulder," says Nguyen. "The important part is pulling your opponent in so you get him nice and tight and then boom, bump him with your shoulder."

Move #2: Ken

"He does a spinning upper cut. He jumps up, connects that upper cut, and then does a spin on the way down," says Nguyen. Felder does this one flawlessly with a paddle.

Move #2: Zangief

This is just a headbutt. "It's pretty self-explanatory, but the important part in re-enacting this is actually in the person reacting. You have to get the timing right for this to really sell," says Nguyen.

Move #3: Karin

Karin does two kicks with a switch-leg move. "Basically she does a front kick without putting her foot down, and she switches to another kick," says Nguyen. "This is a pretty common move in martial arts, so it was really easy to do."

Move #2: F.A.N.G

"He pulls the opponent down, and then does a V-push kick," says Nguyen.
