# Watch Kevin Hart Train Like a Pro Soccer Player

Kevin Hart is man of many talents, from doing standup comedy to acting to staying buff, but he's the first to admit he knows next to nothing about the beautiful game. In the latest episode of his web series What The Fit, Hart recruits YouTuber and fellow soccer novice Emma Chamberlain for a day of drills with the L.A. Galaxy soccer club.

Obviously, soccer players are stereotypically known for their theatrical abilities as well as their athletic prowess, dropping to the ground and performing to the back seats when needed, and so Hart and Chamberlain start things off by learning the all-important "flop" manoeuver from Hollywood stuntman Stuart Wilson.

Then it's time to head over to StubHub Center for the main event; a coaching session with L.A. Galaxy midfielders Joe Corona and Sacha Kljestan, defenders Rolf Feltscher and Daniel Steres, and goalkeeper David Bingham.

The team starts by putting Hart and Chamberlain's footwork to the test with a speed ladder and dribbling exercise. When the pros point out that Hart's technique could do with some finessing, he simply says: "Obviously you guys have never played in Europe."

After that they move onto tackling, where Hart and Chamberlain are able to show off their newly developed "flopping" skills against midfielder Joe Corona. Then it's penalty shootout time. Hart's first two shots go way too wide: "I was trying to bend it like Beckham," he claims. "Too much bend," says Steres.

Hart claws it back though, scoring a goal in the third, fourth and fifth rounds, beating Chamberlain and "tricking" the goalie, before celebrating with all of the swagger and bravado of a seasoned soccer pro.
