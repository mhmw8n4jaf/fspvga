Colby Chilson, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

From New York to Colorado: "I began in healthcare in 2017 after graduating from NYU and began my career on the cardiac floor before moving into labor and delivery. After ten years in New York City, I headed to Colorado to pursue my career in family care. I first worked in primary care at a family medicine clinic, then transitioned into women’s health."

Filling a gap in care: "Through my work in both primary care and women’s health, I quickly realized how limited access to quality perimenopause and menopause care really is. What drew me to Midi was their mission to provide affordable, accessible care to all women, because they deserve nothing less."

Listening as medicine: "With every appointment, I try to make women feel as comfortable as possible. I believe that being kind and truly listening makes all the difference—it builds trust and strengthens the care journey. I meet each patient where she’s at. I show up with a smile, take the time to listen, and create a personalized plan that fits her life."
