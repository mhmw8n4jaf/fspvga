# Jennifer Tran, Midi Clinician

[Midi Clinician](https://allpepay.com/)

[Tran Midi Clinician](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/7068148755.html)

[Jennifer Tran](https://allpepay.com/blog/1450576332.html)

Jennifer Tran, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Specialized expertise for individualized care: “I’m a Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner with three years of experience, specializing in urogynecology and pelvic health. As a pelvic health certified clinician, I focus on the often-overlooked connections between menopause, pelvic function, and overall well-being. Helping women navigate these challenges with the right care and support is what drives my practice.”

Inspired by my mother’s journey: “My approach to care is shaped by my own mother’s menopause experience. She’s in her fifth year, and I’ve watched her struggle despite seeing multiple providers. That’s why I make it a priority to truly hear my patients, working with them to find solutions rather than just throwing treatments at the wall and hoping something sticks. Every woman deserves thoughtful, personalized care that actually makes a difference.”

Celebrating every small victory: “The most rewarding part of my work is seeing the transformation in my patients over time. With each follow-up, I get to witness them moving closer to their goals—whether it’s symptom relief, weight loss, or simply feeling more like themselves again. At Midi, we’re here to support women through every step of their journey, making sure they feel heard, empowered, and well cared for.”
