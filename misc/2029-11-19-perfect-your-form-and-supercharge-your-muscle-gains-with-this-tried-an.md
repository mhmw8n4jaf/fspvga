# Perfect Your Form and Supercharge Your Muscle Gains With this Tried-and-True Tempo-Focused Workout

EVERY SUPERHERO NEEDS a secret weapon. As you endeavor to sculpt your own Marvel-level muscles in Don Saladino’s Superhero Shred program, yours will be this workout. The 27-minute session appears deceivingly easy in comparison to other workouts in the plan: Just five total moves performed for 30 to 45 seconds each.

[this Tried-and-True Tempo-Focused](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/)

But the explosive muscle-building power is all in the tempo. You’ll employ one of Saladino’s go-to rep speeds: 3-1-1. That means you’ll spend three seconds on the lowering phase, one second paused, and one second on the concentric phase. (And remember! That lowering phase, especially as a muscle stretches out, is key to building muscle, according to new research.) You may not squeeze in a high volume of reps, but the quality will be top-tier.
