# Transform Your Body in 7 Days

If you think the beach is a good motivator to get back in shape, just imagine the power of the big screen. "Earlier this year, I returned from a very indulgent trip to Europe to find out that I had exactly one week to get in shape for my next movie, The Stepfather," says actor Penn Badgley, who also plays social outsider Dan Humphry on The CW's Gossip Girl. "What's worse, my character was a swimmer, so I was going to be shirtless in more than half of the scenes."

His solution: Seven days of total-body strength circuits, including the one below. "By the end of the week, I had dropped two to three pounds of fat, and added noticeable muscle," says Badgley. "It was pretty incredible." Run through the following circuit two to four times once or twice a week, moving from one exercise immediately to the next, and resting for two minutes only after you’ve completed them all. Your reward: A brand new body faster than you ever thought possible.

Pull-Up Grab the pull-up bar with an overhand grip that's slightly wider than shoulder width apart, and hang at arm's length. Pull your chest to the bar, pause, and then lower your body back to the starting position. Do as many reps as you can (up to 15).

Leg Raise Grab a chin-up bar with an overhand, shoulder-width grip (or use elbow supports, if available), and hang from the bar with your knees slightly bent and feet together. Simultaneously bend your knees, raise your hips, and curl your lower back underneath you as you lift your thighs toward your chest. Pause when the fronts of your thighs reach your chest, and then slowly lower your legs back to the starting position. Do three to four sets of 8 to 12 reps.

Bosu Ball Pushup Turn a Bosu ball over, so that the half ball portion is on the floor, and position our hands on the sides of this platform. Lower your body until your chest nearly touches the Bosu, pause, and then push yourself back to the starting position as quickly as possible. Do 15 to 20 reps.

Dumbbell Curl to Press Hold a pair of dumbbells at arms length by your sides, palms in. Curl the dumbbells toward your shoulders, and then press the dumbbells above your head until your arms are straight. Reverse the movement to return to the starting position. Do 12 to 15 reps.

Shoulder Fly Grab a pair of dumbbells and bend forward at your hips until your torso is nearly parallel to the floor. Let the dumbbells hang straight down from your shoulders, your palms facing each other. Without moving your torso, raise your arms straight out to your sides until they're in line with your body. Pause, and then slowly return to the starting position. Do 8 to 12 reps.

Arm Raise Grab a pair or dumbbells and let them hang at arm's length by your sides, palms in. Raise your arms straight in front of you until they're parallel to the floor and perpendicular to your torso. Pause, and then slowly lower the dumbbells back to the starting position. Do 8 to 12 reps.

Physio Ball V-Up Lie on the floor, holding a Physio ball between your lower legs, and stretching your arms behind your head. Extend your arms straight up as you simultaneously raise your legs and upper body into a contracted position. Your lower back should remain on the floor, and your legs should be slightly bent. At the top of the movement, transfer the ball from your legs to your hands. Return to the starting position, and then repeat, passing the ball between your hands and feet with each rep. Do 10 to 15 reps.

Dip Grasp the bars of a dip station and lift yourself so that your arms are completely straight. Slowly lower yourself by bending your elbows until your upper arms dip just below your elbows. Pause, and then push back up to the starting position. Do 8 to 12 reps.

Isometric Pushup Assume a pushup position with your arms fully extended, and then lower yourself about halfway to the floor. Hold this position for 30 seconds.

Triceps Extension Grab a pair of dumbbells and stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold the dumbbells at arm's length above your head, your palms facing each other. Without moving your upper arms, lower the dumbbells behind your head. Pause, and then straighten your arms to return to the starting position. Do 8 to 12 reps.
