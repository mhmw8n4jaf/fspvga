About this lesson

Enhance your expertise in lip symmetry and contouring with the Belotero range of products in this comprehensive training video. This step-by-step guide is designed for medical professionals, offering a detailed approach to assessing lip asymmetry and creating a balanced, natural appearance for your patients. The video covers everything from evaluating the proportion of the upper and lower lips to identifying key areas needing correction, such as eversion and vermillion enhancement. With precise injection techniques and shaping methods, you'll learn how to restore symmetry, address asymmetry, and improve lip definition. The video highlights the importance of personalized treatment planning and careful product application to achieve optimal results. By following expert-led instruction, you'll gain the confidence to address common concerns like uneven lip contours and volume discrepancies. This training empowers you to provide customized, aesthetically pleasing lip enhancements, ensuring natural-looking outcomes that exceed patient expectations and elevate your practice’s level of care.

Lip assessment techniques to identify asymmetry and areas needing correction.

Use of Belotero products to enhance volume, contour, and balance in both lips.

Precise injection and molding methods to refine vermillion borders and address fine lines.

Strategies to achieve natural, rejuvenated lip contours with harmonious results.
