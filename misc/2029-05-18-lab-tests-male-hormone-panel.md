What is the Male Hormone Panel?

The Male Hormone Panel is a comprehensive test that measures key hormones affecting men’s health, such as testosterone, cortisol, and estradiol. It helps assess your hormonal balance, which can impact energy, libido, mood, and overall well-being.

Who should get a Male Hormone Panel?

Any man experiencing symptoms like low energy, decreased libido, mood swings, weight gain, or difficulty building muscle should consider getting a hormone panel. It’s also beneficial for those looking to optimize health and performance, particularly as they age.

What hormones are included in the test?

The Male Hormone Panel typically includes:

Testosterone: Regulates sex drive, muscle mass, and bone density.

Free Testosterone: The active form of testosterone that impacts energy and mood.

Estradiol: A form of estrogen that affects weight management, heart health, and libido.

DHEA-S: A precursor to testosterone and estrogen, crucial for energy and stress response.

Cortisol: Known as the "stress hormone," it influences energy levels and weight gain.

What symptoms indicate I may have a hormone imbalance?

Signs of a potential hormone imbalance include:

Fatigue or lack of energy

Low libido or erectile issues

Irritability or mood swings

Difficulty gaining muscle or unexplained muscle loss

Increased body fat or weight gain

Sleep issues or insomnia

How soon will I get my results?

You can expect to receive your results within 7-10 business days after the lab receives your sample.

Can I use the results to start hormone replacement therapy (HRT)?

Yes, the Male Hormone Panel can help determine if hormone replacement therapy is needed. However, you should always consult with a healthcare provider to interpret your results and discuss treatment options.

How often should I test my hormones?

It depends on your symptoms and goals. If you're managing hormone-related health conditions or undergoing treatment like HRT, it’s common to test every 3-6 months. For general wellness, annual testing is usually sufficient.

Can lifestyle changes improve my hormone levels?

Absolutely! Proper diet, regular exercise, stress management, and sufficient sleep can all have a significant impact on your hormonal balance. Your results will provide personalized insights to help guide these changes.
