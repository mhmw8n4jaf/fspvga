What to Consider

Material

Long-sleeve tees are almost exclusively cotton-based, but every now and again, you’ll see blends that help to give the shirt some added softness. But overall, cotton’s the way to go because they’re breathable, soft, and sometimes offer a little stretch to move with you.

Style

Our selection of white tees comes in two styles: standard and pocket. The former is more of a tried and true, clean and basic option. The latter is a more vintage-inspired offering.

[Be Boring These](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/7430690870.html)

Weight

When it comes to weight, lightweight tees (120-180 GSM) are perfect for warmer weather, offering breathability and an airy feel. Midweight tees (180-220 GSM) strike a balance, making them ideal for cooler days and versatile enough for year-round wear. Heavyweight tees (220 GSM and above) are thicker and warmer, great for colder months or layering when you need more insulation.

Fit

It doesn’t matter how good the shirt is if it doesn’t fit properly. When it comes to fits, there are a few common types you’ll come across, each with its own vibe.

Slim Fit

This one’s more tailored and hugs the body, especially around the chest, waist, and arms. It’s perfect for a sharp, streamlined look if you’ve got a trim build or just want something more fitted.

Standard Fit

The go-to for most guys. It’s not too tight, not too loose, and gives you a little more room to move. It’s comfortable and works for just about any body type.

Relaxed Fit

Looser than regular fit, this style gives you extra room throughout the chest, waist, and arms. It’s perfect if you’re after a laid-back, easygoing look or if you want more breathability and comfort.

Boxy Fit

As the name suggests, it’s got a more square, oversized shape. It’s loose and roomy, especially through the torso, and is great for a relaxed, street-style look.

How We Selected

We dug deep into a bunch of long sleeve tees to figure out which ones really delivered on all fronts. First, we looked at material—because the last thing anyone wants is a shirt that’s uncomfortable or feels cheap. We went for ones made from high-quality fabrics, whether it was soft cotton, cotton blends, or something more durable like hemp. Fit was also important—a tee that’s too tight or too loose can throw off your whole vibe, so we made sure to pick those that were tailored in all the right places but still comfy enough to move in.

Our editors, led by Fashion Director Ted Stafford, paid attention to durability—we didn’t want to recommend tees that would lose their shape or softness after a few washes. So we made sure the shirts could hold up to everyday wear, from lounging around to running errands. Finally, we considered the versatility—because you want a long sleeve tee that can be dressed up or down and worn across different seasons. After testing and narrowing it down, we picked the shirts that nailed all of these factors and would make a solid addition to any wardrobe. Read on to see our seven favorites.
