On Stranger Things, Eleven experiences nosebleeds when she's using her powers.

Actress Millie Bobby Brown explained the science behind her character's nosebleeds.

She has to hold the fake blood mixture in her nose until it's action time.

Eleven is one of the most popular characters on Stranger Things, and her shaved head and love of Eggo waffles have led to some creative Halloween costumes ever since the show's premiere in 2016. Another trademark of Eleven is the fact that she gets nosebleeds whenever she uses her powers, and actress Millie Bobby Brown has finally let the world know how she does it.

[to Make Her Nose](https://weight-signal.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/7878825299.html)

Ahead of the Stranger Things season three premiere, Brown appeared on The Late Late Show with James Corden on Tuesday, where she explained that the "blood" is really made of corn syrup. (She sometimes tastes it as she's getting ready for a scene.) As Us Weekly reports, the actress also explained how she holds the mixture in her nose until it's action time on set.

"It’s just a pot of blood and it’s got a squeezy bottle. Then you just lift up your head, you squeeze it in and then whenever you feel it, I put my head down and I say, 'OK guys, everybody ready … roll camera, action!' and then wait."

However, Brown said she sometimes "screws up her timing," and the blood doesn't flow when it's supposed to, leading to a reset and new take.

On the show, Brown frequently has to reach for her squeeze bottle of blood, as her character Eleven experiences a nosebleed pretty much every time she uses her powers. In real life, nose bleeds happen when small blood vessels in the nose burst, but some fans believe that the blood is actually coming from Eleven's brain.
