# 9 Clothing Lines for Guys Who Don't Care About Logos or Labels

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

For too long, we’ve been walking around like human billboards, sporting the iconic symbols of all our favorite brands. And even worse, we’ve been paying major bank for the privilege.

The latest cool-kid designers are keeping their signatures on the DL and our closets are that much better for it.

"We want to have our sneaker designs, rather than a flashy logo, speak for our company’s quality," Oliver Cabell founder Scott Gabrielson says. “We try to create silhouettes, shapes, and a visual language that really stands out, and I think it does that by whispering when everyone else is screaming.”

Gabrielson's monochromatic kicks look designer but they retail at a mid-level price, a major perk of going logo-less, especially for basics like shoes and t-shirts.

Looking to give your wardrobe a blank-slate makeunder? Check out these logo-free pieces from labels with muted, minimalist designs.
