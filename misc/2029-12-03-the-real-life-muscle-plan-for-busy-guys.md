What’s in the Guide

Maingaining works when you balance a slight calorie surplus with hyper-focused training. This is where it changes the game. Most muscle-building training and diet plans ask you to eat several hundred calories above maintenance levels for several weeks or months (your "bulk"), then eat several hundred calories below those levels to "cut" bodyfat later.

A maingaining program lets you eat and train more sustainably: You're eating just 100-200 calories above maintenance. Paired with a nuanced strength training program, this sets you up to build muscle without ever packing on too much fat. The key: Dialing in those calories and finding the optimal training volume to get muscle- and strength-building stimulus.

How the Program Works

With a strategic calorie increase and a balanced progressive overload approach, you’ll stimulate muscle growth without leaving room for fat gain.

WEEKS 1–2

Build Your Foundation

To power your workouts, you’ll increase your daily calories slightly and balance your macros for satiety and muscle growth. In the gym, you’ll focus on hitting two reps in reserve (RIR) on every set to challenge your muscles without risking getting overly sore.

WEEKS 3–5

Increase the Challenge

With your foundation laid, you’ll start to feel stronger—and ready to push harder. On your heavy days, you’ll hit true technical failure on your final set of your first exercise to level-up just enough to spark more muscle growth.

[Muscle Plan for Busy](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/8820587265.html)

WEEKS 4–6

Fine-Tune Your Fueling

By your fourth week you’ll be able to up the intensity by adding a dropset to your first exercise on your volume days, in addition to pushing to technical failure on heavy days. With tougher workouts, you'll want to reassess your diet. If your weight is fluctuating, you’ll adjust your carb intake so you can hit your maintenance weight without sacrificing muscle.

WEEKS 6–8

Crush Your Benchmarks

As you reach the homestretch, you’ll have mastered a variety of muscle-building moves and will be ready to repeat your two RIR sets from Weeks 1 and 2. The only difference this time around? You’ll be able to crank our more reps and push your gains into new territory.
