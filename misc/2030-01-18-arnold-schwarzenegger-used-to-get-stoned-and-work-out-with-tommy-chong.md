# Arnold Schwarzenegger Used to Get Stoned and Work Out With Tommy Chong

[Used to Get Stoned](https://7161777.com/3nixlikh/)

Arnold Schwarzenegger is still upheld as a paragon of drive, discipline and physical excellence even in his seventies, but there was a time when he used to be a little more loosey-goosey in his training regime. During a recent appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live, the actor and bodybuilding legend revealed that he used to be pals with the comedian Tommy Chong, of stoner comedy Cheech & Chong fame.

"You're busting me big time here," Schwarzenegger joked, before telling the story of how he and Chong first met in the early '70s, and become unlikely gym buddies.

"Tommy and I became very good friends," he said. "We both had big, big dreams when we met on Venice Beach, working out there on the outside weightlifting platform, and we talked about how we wanted to be stars, and he wanted to be in comedy and was dreaming about that, and I was dreaming about being the biggest and best bodybuilder and getting into movies and being a leading man, and all of this stuff. So we had a really great time."

"And he was, of course, stoned a lot of times. And as a matter of fact, we went over to a friend's house many times to smoke pot in those days, and he actually taught me to not smoke a whole joint, but have a few hits and try to work out that way. So I started working out with a few hits, before we went to the gym and worked out this way, and it was really fantastic, I should say."

He went on to explain that while he enjoyed this approach to his workouts, getting lit then getting fit, he stopped smoking marijuana as he wanted to focus on progressing his career.

And I then stopped smoking from one day to the next, and didn't smoke a joint ever since."
