Spoilers follow for the Season 2 finale of The White Lotus.

Season 2, Episode 7 of The White Lotus finally revealed the identity of the body that washed ashore in Sicily back in Episode 1, and it is with a heavy heart that fans must bid arrivederci to Tanya, the wealthy oddball played to perfection by Jennifer Coolidge.

In an interview that aired after the season finale, series creator Mike White spoke about the creative decision to kill off a character who has become a fan-favorite, and said that in some ways, the end to Tanya's story was spelled out during her first appearance back in Season 1. That arc revolved around Tanya coming to terms with the death of her mother, with whom she had a complicated relationship, and meeting Greg, who would go on to become her husband in Season 2. White specifically pointed out one line of dialogue from a scene between Tanya and Greg in that first season which foreshadows what would eventually take place in Sicily:

"I've had every kind of treatment over the years. Death is the last immersive experience I haven't tried."

Of course, it's extra ironic that this line was delivered to Greg, who many fans believe to have been in cahoots with the "high end gays" that would attempt to murder Tanya, but as White explains, reckoning mortality has always been a recurring theme for Coolidge's character.

"I was thinking it would be so fun to bring Tanya back, because she's such a great character, but maybe that's the journey for her... a journey to death," he said. "I love her as a character and obviously love Jennifer, but I just felt like, you know, we're going to Italy, she's such a kind of diva, larger than life female archetype, it just felt like we could devise our own operatic conclusion to Tanya's life and her story."

It was also important to White that Tanya not die at the hands of the criminals on the yacht, and that an accidental death at the last moment would be more of a gut-punch to viewers. "It felt like she needed to give her best fight back and that she, in a way had some kind of victory over whoever was conspiring to get rid of her," he said. "It just made me laugh to think like she would like take out this cabal of killers and after she successfully does that she just dies this derpy death and that’s so Tanya."
