# This New Timex x Todd Snyder Watch Will Cost You Less Than $150

A vintage-inspired dial, a sleek, durable strap, and price tag that won’t leave you penniless. The newest rendition from the Timex X Todd Snyder collaboration—dubbed The Blackjack—has the makings of the ideal everyday watch with just the right edge to it.

Snyder, who got his start at GAP, J.Crew, and Polo Ralph Lauren, is continuing his partnership with Timex to produce an impossibly cool collection that somehow never breaks the $150 mark. This latest model is a modern update of a 1970s Timex piece he found in the company’s archives.

The blue and white dial blends inspiration from old-school Formula One odometers and old-school roulette wheels—hobbies we can definitely get into. The rugged nylon NATO strap adds a cool military vibe, as well as sweat-wicking properties—both of which make it perfect for casual occasions. The case is also water-resistant up to 50 meters, so you won’t have to unstrap for late summer pool parties.
