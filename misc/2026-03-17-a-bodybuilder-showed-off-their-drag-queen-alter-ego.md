# A Bodybuilder Showed Off Their Drag Queen Alter Ego

28-year-old Rock Evans has competed in, and won, multiple physique shows, but is not what you might call a traditional bodybuilder. In addition to identifying as nonbinary, they also balance their grueling bodybuilding training by day with a career as a successful drag queen by night: Miss Toto.

"Moving to Miami, I saw everybody being buff and hot, and was like 'I kind of want to do this,' but not really sure what regard or what level or capacity," they say in a recent episode of the Truly webseries Miami Muscle. "For me, bodybuilding started because I wanted that challenge, that drive, and that pageantry that I wasn't getting out of my life."

Throwing themselves into the bodybuilding scene, Evans began to participate in events, and soon found that the things which made them different in this incredibly macho environment also helped them to stand apart. "I try to put my own flare into traditional bodybuilding. The energy I bring to the stage is just different to these hyper-masculine competitors," they say. "In the Miami bodybuilding circuit they probably know me as the gay one, and I've never been met with any adverse reactions. I always leave a physique show with three or four new friends."

These two worlds aren't as vastly different as they first seem, however; Rock explains that they both require an immense amount of personal discipline, and that the physical training of one leads to benefits in the other.

"Bodybuilding and drag, I would say, are very similar, because you're on stage, everyone's looking at you, everyone's judging you. I've worked so hard to put together this package that I'm giving to you, it's changed a lot in the last five years. I work so hard on my physique out of drag, that in drag I want that to carry over."

Ultimately, the most important lessons Rock has learned from both the worlds of drag and bodybuilding have been the payoff of hard work, and the importance of being true to yourself. And while they're currently taking a break from physique competitions during the pandemic, they can't wait to keep chasing their goals.
