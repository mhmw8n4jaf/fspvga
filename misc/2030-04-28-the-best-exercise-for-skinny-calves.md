# The Best Exercise For Skinny Calves

Sure, calf raises are effective for building your calves. But donkey calf raises—a tweaked version of the classic move—work even better, says Men’s Health Fitness Director BJ Gaddour, C.S.C.S.

To perform the exercise, position your feet on a low step and hinge at your hips while you lift and lower your heels instead of standing up straight.
