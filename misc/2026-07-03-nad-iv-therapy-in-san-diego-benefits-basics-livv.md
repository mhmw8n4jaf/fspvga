Frequently asked questions

Does NAD+ decline as we age?

Research across multiple species reports that NAD+ levels tend to fall with age in several tissues, which is one reason it is studied in the context of healthy aging [2].

Is NAD+ IV therapy approved as a treatment for a disease?

NAD+ IV therapy is offered as a wellness service. It is not positioned as a treatment for any medical condition, and you should talk with a qualified provider about your situation.

How is IV NAD+ different from oral NAD+ boosters?

An IV delivers NAD+ into the bloodstream directly, while oral precursors such as NR and NMN are processed through digestion. Research on precursors continues, and it is a separate area from IV delivery.
