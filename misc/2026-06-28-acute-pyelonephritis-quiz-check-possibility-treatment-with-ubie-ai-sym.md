What is Acute Pyelonephritis?

An infection of the kidney typically caused by bacteria.

Typical Symptoms of Acute Pyelonephritis

Symptoms started with lower back pain on one side only

Fever

Burning or painful urination with reduced urine volume

Stomach or abdominal pain in a specific area

Fatigue or low energy that is worse in the morning

Sweating at night requiring change in clothing

Having difficulty urinating or producing less urine

Recurrent fever episodes

Diagnostic Questions for Acute Pyelonephritis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Did you have lower back pain on one side only when your symptoms started?

Do you have a fever?

Have you experienced burning or painful urination along with a decrease in urine volume?

Do you have stomach or abdominal pain in a specific area?

Do you experience fatigue or low energy that is worse in the morning?

Treatment of Acute Pyelonephritis

[Check Possibility Treatment](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Treatment involves antibiotics, which can be taken orally. In more severe cases, hospitalization and intravenous "drip" antibiotics may be necessary. In some instances, a procedure or surgery might be required to drain pus from the kidneys.
