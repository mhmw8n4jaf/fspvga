Stage 4 cirrhosis, also known as decompensated cirrhosis, is the most advanced stage of liver scarring. Common symptoms include:

Fatigue and weakness

Confusion or difficulty concentrating (hepatic encephalopathy)

Swelling in the abdomen (ascites) and legs

Loss of appetite and trouble eating

Jaundice (yellowing of skin or eyes)

These symptoms signal that the liver can no longer function properly, and close management with a healthcare provider is essential.

Because stage 4 cirrhosis symptoms can overlap with other serious conditions, identifying what's driving your symptoms early can make a meaningful difference in your care plan.
