# How to Keep Track of the 2020 CrossFit Games Results

CrossFit has endured a tumultuous year, filled with pandemic-induced shutdowns and a series of controversies that ousted the company's founder and CEO. But finally, after months of planning, the crown jewel of the sport's competitive season is here. The 2020 CrossFit Games is primed for kickoff.

If you're a CrossFit fan, following the Games is a must. Watching the 2020 competition won't be as simple as in years past, however. The event is split into two phases, since a stadium-sized spectacle in Wisconsin was deemed too risky by organizers. The first, a remote competition with athletes around the world submitting video of their workouts, starts this weekend on Friday and Saturday. This will serve as a qualifier for the top five finishers, who will participate in a final competition in Aromas, California in October.

You will be able to watch some of the action and live updates from CrossFit HQ for the first stage—more details on how to do that here—but keeping tabs on favorite athletes and figuring out who's on top might be particularly tricky for fans. Competitors are spread around the world, so the results will be rolling in over the two days of the event, not easy-to-follow head-to-head contests.

If you want the full experience, you do have options. You can watch the live programming and updates from CrossFit HQ using the methods referenced above, and you can follow all of the athletes via this helpful results tracker and leaderboard tool from our friends at BarBend. The guide will be updated after every event, giving you a comprehensive look at the standings.
