What to Consider

Fabric

Lightweight cotton, cotton blends, or twill are great for everyday wear. For more active use, go for nylon or a cotton-nylon-spandex mix—they’re breathable, quick-drying, and offer some stretch for movement. A bit of elastane or spandex (around 1 to 3 percent) can make a big difference in comfort, especially if you’re walking, sitting, or moving around all day.

Pocket Arrangement

Modern cargo shorts often have slimmer, flatter pockets that sit closer to the body—giving you utility without puffiness. Look for zippered or snap-closure pockets to keep valuables secure. Some shorts offer hidden compartments or phone sleeves to help organize your gear. Unless you’re actually camping or on a job site, two to four cargo pockets are more than enough. Too many pockets = bulk and overkill.

Length

7- to 9-inch inseam

A 7- to 9-inch inseam is the sweet spot for most guys—short enough to keep your legs cool and proportionate, but long enough to avoid veering into “too short” territory.

10-inch inseam and higher

10-inch inseams and higher provides extra coverage, falling passed the knee. They tend to look best on taller guys or those with longer legs, since the longer length can overwhelm shorter frames.

Are Cargo Shorts Still Stylish?

Not only are cargo shorts still stylish, they are having a moment right now. All fashion is cyclical, and cargo shorts are one of the items that seem to have peaks and valleys but never really go away entirely. If you like the way cargo shorts look in your wardrobe, you should stick with them no matter what other people are wearing.

Are Cargo Shorts Too Casual?

Overall, cargo shorts are a casual look that should not be worn in an office or any other elevated environment. A good rule to go by is if you’re going to wear leather dress shoes and a collared shirt, you should not wear cargo shorts. However, you can certainly wear them in any casual environment. Think of how you wear chino shorts with a T-shirt, polo shirt, or sweatshirt and just swap in cargo shorts.
