What are the symptoms of urinary retention?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Urinary retention is a condition where you cannot fully empty your bladder. Common symptoms include difficulty starting urination, a weak or interrupted stream, straining to pee, and feeling the urge to urinate again shortly after finishing. It can develop suddenly (acute) or gradually (chronic), and causes range from an enlarged prostate or nerve issues to medication side effects or urinary tract infections.

Because urinary retention can signal an underlying condition that may worsen without treatment, understanding your symptoms early is important.
