# The 100 Squat Fitness Challenge

Like walking and jumping, squatting is one of the most natural human movements. But that doesn't mean it's easy—especially when you perform 100 repetitions as fast as possible like you will during this week's fitness test. "The speedy repetitions will challenge your heart, lungs, and muscles, and stoke your metabolism for hours afterward," says Todd Durkin, author of The IMPACT! Body Plan and the creator of this challenge.

[The Squat Fitness](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Here's how to do it: Start a stopwatch, and then stand with your feet slightly wider than shoulder-width apart, toes forward. Push your hips back as if you’re about to sit in a chair, and lower your body until your thighs are parallel to the floor. Quickly push back up to the starting position. That's 1 rep. Do 100. You can stop and rest when you need to, but keep the clock running the entire time.Ready to give it a shot? Watch the video above to see Durkin demonstrate the challenge. He completed 100 squats in 2 minutes and 37 seconds. What was your time? Tell us in the comments below.
