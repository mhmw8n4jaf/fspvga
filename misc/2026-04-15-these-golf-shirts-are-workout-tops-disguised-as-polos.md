What to Consider

Stretch

The most vital element you should demand in a golf shirt is a stretchy, comfortable fabric. Without stretch, you’ll find yourself feeling too restricted and having a hard time when putting, swinging, or bending down a bit—very similar to the needs for stretch in workout shirts. Stretch fabric, usually made with spandex or elastane, allows a golf shirt to have a four-way stretch and move with your motions when you play. Keep in mind that the primary fabric material in many golf shirts, polyester, isn’t necessarily stretchy since it has no elasticity on its own, so it’s often blended with spandex or elastane to bring out a fuller range of motion. Or, a 100% polyester fabric can be reworked to be stretchy itself, such as via knitting or crimping.

Performance

Besides stretch, golf shirts, as all outdoor and workout clothes, need to have other activewear functionalities such as moisture-wicking, quick-drying, and breathability to keep you cool during the heat. These anti-humid features are usually courtesy of activewear materials like polyester, which is hydrophobic and water-repellent. And if it gets too hot, you’ll want to look for breathability offered by nylon, such as in Malbon's Performance Pique Polo. Additionally, when the sun is really blazing, many golf shirts now offer UV protection and built-in SPF or UPF ratings to shield your skin from harmful sun rays during long hours on the course. And lastly, the fabric of golf shirts should be seriously lightweight to not let you feel weighed down.

Fit

A well-fitted shirt not only makes you look better but also allows for unrestricted movement and flexibility. In this case, most golf shirts come in a standard or classic fit that's roomy through the chest to the waist and cut for a lower armhole and a fuller sleeve that falls closer to the elbow. This fit skims the body without being too tight or too loose and offers maximum coverage against the sun and weather. The best golf shirts should also have enough room in the shoulders and arms to accommodate your swings while maintaining a neat and polished look. Also, pay attention to the overall length of golf shirts to see if they can be tucked in.

What Is the Best Fabric for Golf Shirts?

The best fabric for golf shirts is typically a blend of polyester or nylon with spandex or elastane. This blend offers a balance of breathability, softness, stretch, and moisture-wicking properties. Polyester and nylon are performance materials that offer moisture management, allowing sweat to evaporate quickly, and can be made to be breathable through different knitting techniques. Spandex or elastane offers stretch to ensure the fabric moves with you. This fabric in golf shirts keeps golfers cool, dry, and comfortable during their rounds.

Are Golf Shirts Business Casual?

While golf shirts were designed with golf in mind, their comfort, breathability, and style have made them popular beyond the course. Golf shirts are now versatile enough to be worn in various casual settings, specifically business casual occasions. Because of their appearance, golf polos will easily elevate your everyday fit.

How to Style Golf Shirts?

On the course, pair your golf shirt with appropriate bottoms such as golf shorts or golf pants, and tuck in your shirt for a more polished and traditional look—or when at a country club that requires it. Off the course, pair golf shirts with casual shorts, chinos, or jeans for a relaxed and comfortable fit, and in this case you can wear golf shirts untucked for a more laid-back look. For dressing up—and you can indeed dress up in golf shirts, choose a golf shirt in a solid color or a subtle pattern, pair it with tailored trousers or khakis, and layer it under a blazer or a sports coat.
