How to Care for Flannel-Lined Jeans

Like every other garment, make sure to check the instructions for the best care on the inside tag. Most flannel-lined jeans can be machine washed, but we suggest turning them inside out and using a mild detergent. You can toss them in the dryer on a low heat temperature or allow them to air dry to maintain their quality.

What To Look For in Flannel-Lined Jeans

The main purpose of flannel-lined jeans is to provide maximum warmth during the colder months. Choosing the best pair depends on preference as well as some a few other important factors.

Weight of Fleece: The level of warmth relies heavily on the weight of the fleece lining. So, if you're looking for a lightweight feel that works better than your typical denim trousers, then go with a thin layer of flannel lining that's soft, but still capable of protecting you from the wind. Mid-weight fleece offer versatility and optimal comfort, without adding unnecessary any bulk. To achieve sweatpants-level insulation, opt for faux-shearling-lined jeans.

Fit and Rise: Low-rise jeans tend to flatter a thinner physique, but not provide the best coverage in brisk temperatures. Mid-rise jeans are more classic and easier to style with different tops like sweaters and tuck-in shirts. High-waisted styles provide the most coverage around your waistline and lower back. But they're likely to bunch more around the midsection and create unwanted bulk.
