EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT THE FAST AND LEAN WORKOUT PLAN

Imagine each workout as a giant “wheel,” with 6 exercises total. One of those moves sits in the middle of the wheel. That’s your hub. The other 5 moves—the spokes—span out from your hub.

Why a wheel? Because the training template burns fat fast and improves your aerobic fitness.

Every workout is done at a max intensity, which raises your heart rate, dilates your blood vessels, and improves your cardiovascular system better than slow-and steady running. They’ll also keep your body’s natural fat burners stoked all day long.

The Hub This is your main exercise. It’s typically a cardio-intensive total-body exercise that’s done at a high speed so your heart rate stays elevated throughout the workout.

You’ll do this movement before every spoke. By always going back to your hub, you’ll create metabolic stress the whole time, burning fat and increasing your aerobic fitness.

The Spokes Think of the five spokes as accessory exercises to your hub.

You’ll flow between them—from the hub to a spoke to the hub to another spoke—the entire workout, hitting each spoke once.

How to Customize the Program

If you are new to exercise, getting back in shape, or find this program too demanding, do 2 to 3 workouts a week, resting one day between each workout. You can choose whichever wheels you like.

Add a workout a week every 2 weeks until you’re doing the prescribed 6 workouts a week.
