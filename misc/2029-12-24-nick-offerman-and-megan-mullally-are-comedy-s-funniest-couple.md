One funny person? Great. Two funny people? Even better. But two funny people in a relationship? Well, that's as close to perfect as you can get. Nick Offerman and Megan Mullally are definitely an example of that amazing comedic couple that somehow manage to blend their love for each other with their terrific senses of humor, and the films and TV shows they've appeared in together only further prove that.

Offerman's new role on Devs will see him depart from comedy, and he plays Forest, CEO of quantum computing company Amaya. And while it sounds pretty straightforward, expect intrigue, drama, and even a possible murder.

To celebrate his new role, we decided to take a look back on Offerman and Mullally's relationship—here's what you need to know about comedy's funniest couple.

How did Nick Offerman meet Megan Mullally?

Offerman and Mullally met in Los Angeles in 2000 while they were both acting in a play called The Berlin Circle, and the couple both told Buzzfeed that they had an instant connection to each other.

Mullally explained, "I think we were the only two non-company members. It was our first time working with that theater company. We had a lot of scenes together, and I started thinking, 'Wait a minute, he's funny.' And then I started thinking, 'Wait a minute, is he cute?' What's happening?"

Offerman added, "I remember thinking, 'Wait a minute, is she a fucking genius? Wait a minute, is she looking at me? Grrrrr! She good-lookin.'"

Mullally was 41 at the time, and she assumed that Offerman was in his late 30s. However, he was actually just 29, and she told GQ that was she was upset when she found out his real age: "I thought he looked old! And he was mature, so when I found out he was 29, I said these two words: You motherf*****. And I almost ran my car off the road."

They started dating soon after that, and Offerman actually appeared in a guest role on Will & Grace in 2001.

Offerman and Mullally stunned fans (and their friends) when they threw a surprise wedding in September 2003, about 18 months after they started dating. Their wedding was actually the day before Emmys, so their 20 guests—which included Mullally's Will & Grace co-stars Eric McCormack, Sean Hayes and Debra Messing—thought that they were just attending a pre-Emmys party.

At the actual Emmys the next day, Mullally wore the white bustier top and pants that she got married in.

In 2013, Mullally and Offerman participated in an online Q&A for The AV Club, and Offerman said that the two decided to get married because they "fell in love, and before long, we could just tell that we wanted to stay together and make a life together. We wanted to declare to each other and our friends and family that we were in it to win it. It’s important to note that it was not the result of any planning on either of our parts. In fact, we were both looking rather in the other direction when we ran smack into it."

Are Nick Offerman and Megan Mullally still married?

Offerman and Mullally are still happily married, and the world is definitely a better place for it. The couple also works together pretty frequently: Offerman made another appearance on the Will & Grace reboot in 2018, while Mullally played Offerman's ex-wife in multiple episodes of Parks and Recreation. They've also appeared together in The Kings of Summer and Smashed, and they performed voice work together in Bob’s Burgers and Hotel Transylvania 2. 2014 saw them go on a raunchy live comedy tour together, and in 2018, they released The Greatest Love Story Ever Told, a book about their romance. It soon became a New York Times Bestseller.

The couple told New York Magazine that they have a rule that they can't spend more than two weeks apart, and they say that working and living together isn't a problem for them at all.

What else are Nick Offerman and Megan Mullaly up to nowadays?

In 2019, the couple started their own podcast called on the EarWolf network called In Bed With Nick and Megan. The show features the two of them in conversation with some of their famous friends (the first episode has Bill Hader) in...well, their bed.

The couple also, of course, are kept busy by their work. Since Parks and Recreation, Offerman has kept busy with some against-type work in shows like Devs and Fargo, and movies like Hearts Beat Loud and Bad Times at the El Royale. In addition to her work on the Will and Grace reboot, Mullally has also had roles in movies like Richard Linklater's Where'd You Go, Bernadette? and The Disaster Artist.

Do Nick Offerman and Megan Mullally have children?

Offerman and Mullally don't have children, a decision that they opened up to GQ about in 2017.

"I never had a burning desire to have children. But then I met Nick, and I thought 'This is the only person I’d do this with.' So we tried, but I was a little long in the tooth for that sort of thing," Mullally explained. "But we didn’t turn it into a soap opera. We tried for about a year or so, and it didn’t happen, and took that to mean it wasn’t meant to be."

But when they aren't working, Mullally told People that they like to stay home with their dogs and binge watch TV. Their favorite shows? The Bachelor and Survivor.
