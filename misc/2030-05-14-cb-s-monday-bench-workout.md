# CB's Monday Bench Workout

The good news? I didn't have to drive anywhere in todays ice storm...and my dog, a 1-year old Chocolate Lab, loves playing in the first snowfall he's ever experienced.

The bad news? I'm a little sore from the 8 hours of exercise filming we did on the weekend for some future projects.

But the show must go on...so here's todays workout:

[Bench Workout](https://neurocurrent.china-akan.com/)

1) Snatch (3x2)

2a) Bench press (5x5)- And here's where things went sour...something was tight (my right pec minor, I think) from the weekend photoshoot, so I stopped after my last warmup set of 225- I will either try to bench again on wednesday or wait till next week

2b) powerblock split squat (3x8) - did all of these, not sure of the weight, maybe 55 pounds per powerblock?

3a) powerblock incline press (3xMax) - skipped these as well

3b) powerblock elbows out row (5xMax) - used the full weight of the powerblocks...I think they are 85 pounds. I added 2 sets to this exercise because I couldn't do some of the other exercises. Got 8-10 reps per set.

My friend, and NYC/New Jersey strength Jason Ferruggia has a great post aboot overtraining on his blog: http://www.jasonferruggia.blogspot.com/

Read it. I've used his advice to help plan my workouts and switched to a 3-day per week, total body training system because of some of our discussions.

Jay specializes in training MMA guys (http://www.combatconditioningsecrets.com/) and hardgainers (TheHardgainer.com). Jay writes articles for T-Mag all the time, and his blog posts will really fire you up to train, particularly if you are a former athlete.

Recently, I've also bought into two ideas from Strength Coach Mike Boyle:

A) most people shouldn't have shrugs in their workout - if you sit at a desk or drive a lot, you probably have oiverworked traps...so you need other exercises for the low and mid-traps

B) for athletes, there's no reason to go over 8 reps.
