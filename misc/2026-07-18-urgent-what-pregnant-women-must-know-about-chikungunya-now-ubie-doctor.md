What should pregnant women know about Chikungunya virus?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Chikungunya virus is a mosquito-borne illness that can cause high fever and severe joint pain in pregnant women. While most cases resolve on their own, infection during pregnancy—especially near delivery—may pose risks to the baby, including mother-to-child transmission and complications for newborns. Pregnant women experiencing fever, joint pain, rash, or muscle aches after possible mosquito exposure should seek medical evaluation promptly.
