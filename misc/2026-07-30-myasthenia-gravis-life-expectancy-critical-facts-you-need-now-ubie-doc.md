Internal Medicine

Myasthenia gravis (MG) is a chronic autoimmune disorder that can impact life expectancy, particularly during severe flare-ups such as a myasthenic crisis. However, thanks to advances in treatment—including immunosuppressants, thymectomy, and targeted therapies—most people with MG today live a normal or near-normal lifespan. Early diagnosis, consistent medical care, and lifestyle adjustments play a critical role in long-term outcomes.
