Heads up, Lucifer fans: The Lord of Hell is coming back for you, and he might be closer than you think.

After months of wondering exactly when Tom Ellis would be gracing television screens once again, Netflix confirmed on Monday that the first part of the show’s highly-anticipated fifth season will be coming to the streaming site on August 21.

But in typical Lucifer fashion, Netflix couldn’t just simply reveal its premiere date—that would be too simple. Instead, the network gave fans a thirst trap-worthy video compilation of the show’s “hottest, sexiest, most downright devilish” moments… a perfect 66.6 seconds of them.

Though the video does show off plenty of moments featuring Ellis’ impressive physique throughout the show's last four seasons, it sadly doesn’t give any snippets from the upcoming set of episodes. Netflix did, however, give some insight to Deadline about what fans could expect to see:

“In the fifth season, the stakes are higher than ever. Secrets will be revealed, beloved characters will die, and we’ll finally get an answer to the question, ‘will they or won’t they?’”
