# Watch a Pro Bodybuilder Try a Strongman Workout with Martins Licis

Reigning World's Strongest Man Martins Licis is sitting out this year's competition due to injuries sustained after he was hit by a car while on his bike, but that doesn't mean he's been skimping on his training. He recently took on a CrossFit workout with pro Chandler Smith and trained with Army Green Berets. And now he's sharing a recent training session with pro bodybuilder Eric Janicki.

This isn't just a random YouTube collab between some big dudes, either. Janicki and Licis go back. "We used to be co-workers at a place called Ultra Body Fitness in Hollywood," says Licis. "We've never really worked out together."

So Licis decides to show the bodybuilder the ropes on some strongman events. They start their workout outside with a keg toss (using a sand bag weight in place of real kegs.) Licis reps out an impressive 10 tosses in a row.

[Strongman Workout with Martins](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/blog/7745072475.html)

Next, they head into the gym to work on a heavy single-arm dumbbell press. Janicki grabs a heavy dumbbell to try this move out, with Licis looking on at his form. Licis takes a turn, grabbing a 140 pound dumbbell and nailing two reps with his left arm. He switches to the right, but stops the rep before it starts to keep himself safe (he notes that he has some nerve damage on his right side). Janicki tries the weight right after.

"Come on, Eric! This one's big! It's unwieldy! It's awkward!" says Licis. "Keep that positioning, let's go! Tight upper back, tight upper back." His final cue is to squat slowly, then jump hard to press the weight. Janicki can't quite get it raised.

"I got it off my shoulder!" laughs Janicki.

Next, they head outside for the farmer's walk.

"I have never done this," says Janicki before he tries it out successfully.

Licis also gives it a go, carrying 175 pounds. The guys then load the carry with 225 pounds and time each other doing a carry. Licis clocks in at 6.2, and Janicki clocks in at 7.9 seconds. Then they load it to 275 pounds and do another round, and Licis clocks in at 5.9 seconds.

"That thing felt heavy, I wasn't focusing on speed. The thing i was thinking about was my hands and 'thinking don't let go'," says Janicki.

Finally, they hit the Atlas stone. Licis starts and has a goal of seven reps, then Janicki also tries.

"We just lost a few years off our lives today, but we had fun doing it," says Licis after the workout.
