It’s official: Getting a professional wax has made its way onto the must-do grooming list for dudes. Unibrow giving you grief? Weird little ear hair on the rise? Stubble out of control? Especially when you consider waxing will give you back all that time you spend in the bathroom plucking and preening. By opting for a professional wax rather than trying to navigate tweezers (hello, ingrown hairs!), it's claimed your hair could take a few weeks to grow back.

Few things are more annoying than ingrown hair. But there’s more good news: Waxing also goes to work on these too. “Some people are more prone to ingrown hairs than others, depending on the way that their hair grows out of the follicle. If you’re somebody who struggles with in-grown hairs after shaving, then definitely try to switch to waxing, because when we wax, we’re pulling the hair out by the root, as opposed to just cutting it off at the surface,” Green says.
