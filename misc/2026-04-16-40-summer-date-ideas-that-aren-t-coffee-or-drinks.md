# 40 Summer Date Ideas That Aren't Coffee or Drinks

[That Aren't Coffee or](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/6577829304.html)

It's finally summertime, which brings opportunities for outdoor fun and new ways to connect with other singles. Don't get us wrong, dinner and drink dates are totally cool, but they can get a little old, not to mention expensive. The great thing about planning a summer date is that the whole outdoor world is your oyster. (Just remember to stock up on moisture-wicking underwear to keep things as fresh as possible down there in the heat.)

As you determine which summer date idea works best, you’re gonna want to take some time to plan, because making an effort will show your date how much you care—trust. We're not saying you should hire a skywriter for a first date or anything—that would be a little much; we simply urge you not to wait until the last minute to frantically scour Google Maps for a bar that's equidistant between your homes. You can do better than that. Plus, knowing what you’ll do a few days in advance can build anticipation, adds relationship coach Lee Wilson. Planning ahead makes your next date “a special day,” rather than something typical, he says—and that can make things more exciting.

Bonus idea: Surprise your date when it comes to the details, Wilson suggests. Assuming your date is flexible, you tell them the basics, like the time and what to wear, but don’t give everything away. This can be especially great if you’re in a committed relationship and used to a routine.

Ready to get out there? Here are 40 ways to heat things up this summer.

Go for a Go-Kart

A summer trip around the track is perfect. Search for high- or low-speed options, and make reservations if you can.

Try Ziplining.

Forget terrible pick-up lines and try an actual line instead. Search for options at adventure parks or resorts (if your area has them), or start at www.ziplinerider.com.

Charter a Boat

Water fans may go wild for this option. And if you have a designated captain, party it up (safely) for a '90s hip-hop vibe.

Get Down on the Farm

Go fruit or flower picking at a local spot. You can feed your date strawberries or give them a bouquet to create memories.

Hit the Pool

Yeah, it’s an excuse to strip down. For a playful trip, try a city spot. For a luxe experience, try a private pool, like one at a nice hotel.

Go to the Beach

But visit at dusk to catch the sunset as it glints on the water.

Channel Your Childhood

Get nostalgic when you go to a playground. Holding hands? Optional.

Park Yourselves at a Park

It’s a great place to have a conversation. And we’re not saying to bring adult beverages but, if you do, keep it quiet.

Take in the Night Sky

You can appreciate the view whenever. But on July 23, you can meet under the full moon, aka the Buck Moon. (Yes, that’s the real name). Or go on August 12 or 13 to catch the Perseids Meteor Shower. (Find more astronomical events here.)

Take a Hike

But stay together. Bonus points if you pack a lunch and find a place your date’s never visited.

Rent a Horse and Carriage

They’re not just for weddings. Wilson notes carriage sightings can be common in Nashville; you can search for vendors in your local city or check sites like Viator.com or gigsalad.com.

Go Horseback Riding

You can hop onto the saddle whether you’re beginners or more advanced. Start your search for lessons at local farms or ranches to star in your own Western.

Visit a Theme Park

Get close when you ride a roller coaster or a log flume at a park near you. Or flashback to your childhood (again) with a trip to a park like LEGOLAND New York. It features seven themed lands, including more than 15,000 models built out of more than 30 million LEGO bricks (many of them life-sized), and The Dragon Coaster ride. (See other locations here.)

Cozy Up at a Carnival or Boardwalk

If one isn’t nearby, it may be worth a short trip. You can almost taste the pizza and popcorn right now.

Wind Down at a Winery

Though fall can bring harvest season, wineries can hold special events during the summer, including musical performances and tastings. Check locations near you.

Go to the Zoo

Take a Cooking Class

Why should dudes on Food Network have all the fun? Find in-person or virtual options on eventbrite.com. (Disclosure: This reporter’s a contributor there, too.) Or check with local cooking schools.

Go to a Golf Course

Wilson also suggests a driving range. Either way, you can show off your hand-eye coordination skills, or let your date shine with their approach.

Pull Up to Mini Golf

If you want something with a little more color, check out a mini golf course. No need to chase windmills when you can find one together.

Take Flight

On a hot-air balloon, of course. Book a tour and appreciate the view at sunset or sunrise (for the early birds).
