Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A blend of turmeric, ginger, cinnamon, and honey may help reduce inflammation in the body. Current research most strongly supports the anti-inflammatory effects of turmeric (curcumin) and cinnamon, while ginger and honey are widely recognized for complementary antioxidant and soothing properties.

Key points:

Turmeric contains curcumin, shown in studies to lower inflammatory markers.

Cinnamon has demonstrated anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects.

Ginger and honey are traditionally used to support immune and digestive health.

While these natural ingredients may offer benefits, inflammation can also signal an underlying health issue that deserves attention.
