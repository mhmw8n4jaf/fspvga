Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Older adults should see a doctor about their sleep problems if they regularly have trouble falling asleep, wake up often during the night, or feel excessively tired during the day. These symptoms may signal an underlying sleep disorder, medication side effect, or health condition that needs medical evaluation.

Persistent sleep issues in older adults are not just a normal part of aging—they can affect memory, mood, balance, and overall health. Identifying the cause early leads to better treatment outcomes and improved quality of life.
