What is a lipoma?

A lipoma is a lump under the skin caused by an overgrowth of fat cells, according to Medical News Today. It's still unclear why some people get lipomas, but genetics, other existing medical conditions, and injuries could play a role. You don't necessarily have to remove a lipoma, but may want to if it meets certain criteria.
