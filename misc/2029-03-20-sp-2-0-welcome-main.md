How does Superpower curate its supplement & prescription offerings?

Most health marketplaces optimize for shelf space. We optimize for outcomes. Every product in the Superpower marketplace is chosen to avoid the most common reasons supplements fail in the real world.

We screen for correct dosage, not label claims

Many supplements are under-dosed to look cheaper and are not optimized for results. For example:

Vitamin D products often contain ~500-1,000 IUs

Most people need closer to 2,000-4,000 IU based on their baseline test results

We only include products with dosages that align with evidence and are adjustable to your actual needs.

We choose bioavailable forms

Not all ingredients are absorbed the same way. Common issues we screen out:

Inactive or poorly absorbed forms of B12, folate, and minerals

Forms chosen for cost, not effectiveness

Products are selected for forms your body can actually use, not just pass through.

We account for cofactors and interactions

Supplements don't work in isolation. We curate products with proper cofactors and recommend combinations that make sense together - so nothing you take works against something else. Examples:

Vitamin D + Vitamin K2: Vitamin D is more effective when paired with K2, which helpsdirect calcium to bones rather than soft tissue. We recommend them together.

Zinc + Copper: Zinc and copper compete for absorption. High-dose zinc can deplete copper over time. We account for copper levels when recommending zinc.
