# Stop Doing These Glute Exercises. Do These 5 Moves Instead.

THERE ARE OVER a dozen muscles in your legs, but if you find yourself caught inside of fitness social media's algorithmic echo chamber, there's just one that gets most of the attention: the glutes.

"The new frontier of lower body training these days is all about your glutes," says Ebenezer Samuel, C.S.C.S., MH fitness director. "That means a host of Instagram influencer types that are going to deliver you new and 'innovative' ways to train your glutes."

[Doing These Glute](https://weight-signal.sunsimiaosd.com/)

These so-called "innovations" aren't always as effective as influencers make them out to be—and if you fill your training plan with nothing but flashy moves you see on TikTok, you won't get the gains you're aiming for with your workouts. Here, Samuel explains which glute-focused exercises are not worth your time, and what to replace them with.

5 Glute Exercises You Should Stop Doing

Heel Press/Bird Dog

The heel press is a variation of the bird dog movement that's great in some regards (think core bracing)—but not for muscle growth in the glutes. The move is excellent for beginners to build base strength, and for use as a warm up to activate the glutes. It challenges your glutes in the stretched position, but ultimately, it doesn't provide enough stimulus to the muscle to spark the growth you're looking for.

Do This Instead: Hip Thrusts

The classic hip thrust presents a similar idea of challenging the glutes in a stretched position. With the hip thrust, though, you're able to really load up weight, which is what will ultimately drive muscle growth. The glutes are some of the strongest muscles in the body, so don't be afraid to stack on the plates.

Single-Leg Deadlift

Don't get us wrong: the single-leg deadlift is a great exercise if you're looking to improve your balance and coordination. But, you're not really going to be able to load up this move enough to grow significant muscle.

Do This Instead: Loaded Step-Up

The step-up still challenges single-leg strength through hip extension, while allowing more opportunity to load up the movement. You're also able to change up the amount of stretch on your glutes based on the height of the box or bench you use.

Glute Bridge

[Stop Doing These](https://lifegoesom.com/zt3tpb5/)

The glute bridge is another great body weight or warm-up movement, but that's about where its benefits stop. You just can't load it the way we need to in order to stimulate muscle growth, and we aren't putting any stimulus through the glute in the stretched position.

Do This Instead: Hip Thrust

The effectiveness of the hip thrust bears repeating here. You're able to find that stretch at the bottom of the movement, unlike in the glute bridge. And, you're able to stack on the plates for that full squeeze at the top of the motion.

Cable Deadlift

The cable deadlift is ultra-popular among the influencer crowd, Samuel says. In theory, this move adds some load to the glutes while they're stretched in the hinge. In reality, though, the direction and angle of the load really cause more of your back and shoulders to stretch rather than your glutes.

[These Glute Exercises](https://mimi567.com/lhk64ua/)

Do This Instead: Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

Don't mess with the classics. The dumbbell RDL loads your glutes in that stretch position at the correct angle. This is another movement that you can really work with a lot of weight, too.

"That load is what is really going to drive your glutes towards the strength and muscle that you really want," Samuel says.
