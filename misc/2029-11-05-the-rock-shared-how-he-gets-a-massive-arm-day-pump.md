# The Rock Shared How He Gets a Massive Arm Day Pump

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson frequently refers to his aspiration to be the "hardest worker in the room," and in a new post on his Instagram story he elaborated a little on how his training plays into that.

The actor, producer and entrepreneur uploaded a video of himself performing a series of barbell curls as part of his arm day routine during a Monday night workout in his Iron Paradise, and explained that pushing himself to the point of physical discomfort and beyond helps him build "momentum" for the week ahead.

"Pushed (and pulled) hard tonight after a long day and was tired as hell but had to set the tone for the work week with some good momentum," Johnson wrote in the caption. "Living in that 'uncomfortable zone' is always a good place be be - for any business or industry you’re in or whatever goals you need to accomplish. Set your tone with strong intention and have a productive week, my friends."

Johnson is using an EZ bar here, which enables him to target his biceps. The EZ bar is a popular lifting option as it provides a number of different grip options, as Johnson demonstrates in the video when he switches up his grip towards the end of the session, moving it inwards so that the stimulus from the exercise hits the peak of the bicep. This essentially counts as a dropset, allowing Johnson to eke out a handful of extra reps and get the absolute most out of the workout.
