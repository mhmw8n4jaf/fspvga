# There Are So Many Naked People in ‘Hollywood', It Shocked One of the Actors

Ryan Murphy's latest Netflix drama Hollywood drops tomorrow; inspired by actual people and events in showbiz history, it promises to be a tale of ambition and seduction. Emphasis on the latter. According to Jim Parsons, who plays real-life talent agent Henry Willson, the show features so much nudity he found it "awkward and shocking" to film one scene.

"I've gone to a lot of gay clubs in my time, and I've never seen that many naked people at once, ever," Parsons told Ellen DeGeneres over video chat, referring to a decadent sequence in a swimming pool. "I don’t think that's legal in most places... We were surrounded by so many naked people. I just kept trying not to look, but there really came a point – I mean, it was hours we were doing this – I stopped not trying to look at some points."

He went on to say that he soon got over his own reservations, and respected his fellow performers for their openness with their own bodies.

"I was just like, you know what? What I really feel is admiration," he said. "To have that kind of relationship with your body that you’re able to just walk around naked in front of this huge crew, and all these other actors and everything, in and out of the swimming pool, I was like, 'I am in awe.'"

Best known for playing eccentric genius Sheldon Cooper in the long-running sitcom The Big Bang Theory, Parsons is about to give audiences a completely different performance as the closeted Willson. Hollywood marks his second collaboration with Ryan Murphy, following his role in the HBO adapatation of Larry Kramer's play The Normal Heart. He will star in Netflix's version of The Boys in the Band, also produced by Murphy, later this year.
