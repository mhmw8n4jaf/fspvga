# Here's How Sebastian Stan and Anthony Mackie Became BFFs

Whether it's causing trouble on their press tours or staring intensely into each others eyes, Sebastian Stan and Anthony Mackie have the kind of friendship that most of us only dream of, and fans will get to see more of the the duo in the upcoming Disney+ series The Falcon and the Winter Soldier.

Stan appears on our January/February cover, and when journalist Lauren Larson called Mackie to get some inside info on the famously private actor, Mackie relayed the following: "I don’t know what the male equivalent would be of 'resting bitch face,' but Sebastian has nailed that 100 percent."

[Stan and](https://3899241.com/18m67a44/)

Stan and Mackie first starred together in 2014's Captain America: The Winter Soldier, but fans may be surprised to hear that the duo didn't hit it off when they first met—all they did was shake hands. Stan's a lot quieter than the naturally gregarious Mackie, but after spending some more time together on the press tour for The Winter Soldier, they became the best buds we know and love today. Those same press tours draw fans to the duo today, as Stan and Mackie are known to dance, compliment each other, and drag Tom Holland during interviews.

Mackie often pulls Stan out of his shell, including one memorable night during a press tour in Beijing. "It [their night out] just went on and on and on. We had to do press the next morning, and he’s like, 'I'm going to bed.' I’m like, 'Nope,'" Mackie told Men's Health. "I took his wallet and his cell phone so he couldn’t get into his hotel room. Then, by the time we got to the press, I was fine. He just looked like he’d gotten hit by a car."
