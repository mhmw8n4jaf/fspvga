# Ralph Fiennes Became Gym Pals With His ‘28 Years Later’ Infected Nemesis

AS IS THE case with just about every movie he's in, Ralph Fiennes is one of the very best parts of 28 Years Later. In the new horror-thriller from writer Alex Garland and director Danny Boyle, Fiennes plays Dr. Kelson, a mysterious character who enters in the film's final 30 minutes and comes close to stealing it. While Kelson has a frightening appearance—he's covered in red-tinted iodine and looks appropriately insane for what is an insane world we're spending time in—he's actually the most emotionally grounded character in the whole movie.

But given that the film is taking place 28 years into the massive outbreak of a Rage Virus that turns humans into zombie-esque monsters, he also has a good grip on how to handle these guys. One of the 28 Years Later's great creations is a new type of infected known as an "Alpha," which are essentially monsters that are bigger, stronger, and faster than any others. We see throughout the movie that Alphas have the signature move of ripping heads off with the spinal cord intact, and also running around naked and letting their manhoods flop around.

Kelson has run in with a certain Alpha often, so much so that he knows exactly how to sedate it (his medical background coming in handy), and has given it a name: Samson. Kelson and Samson have a couple run-ins throughout the film, including in Fiennes's final scene. It's a tense moment in a film full of them.

Fiennes and the Alpha aren't adversaries when the camera isn't rolling, however. In fact, they've become close pals and often hit the gym together.

"Me and Ralph have become best buds," actor Chi Lewis-Parry, who plays Samson, told Variety in an interview. "We go to the gym and work out together a lot."

Lewis-Parry went on to explain that he was a longtime fan of Fiennes's work, and that one of his earliest jobs in the film industry was working as a stand-in for Hagrid on one of the Harry Potter films. He remembered admiring the work Fiennes was doing in those movies (He plays the franchise's chief villain, Voldemort), and shared this memory with Fiennes on the set of 28 Years Later. It instantly brought them close.

"He’s a very special man and I love him dearly," Lewis-Parry said. "And I truly mean that he’s done so much for me just by being present in any moment. He asks how you are, he supports you if you need something."

This isn't the first time that Fiennes's impressive physical prowess has come up. At 62, he's proven to be in tip-top shape, and last year his shredded body for his role in the film The Return went viral.

As it turns out, his intense training is something of a pattern.

"I’m not joking with you, but I can’t keep up with him," Lewis-Parry said. "He’s in tremendous shape and he never complains and he’ll always push to his limit. We trained a couple of weeks ago and I said, 'Dude, you’re inspiring. Because you’re much older than me and you’re not just keeping up, you’re setting the pace.'"

Hopefully both guys keep their gym sessions up—and we get to see them face off again in the next 28 Years Later movie.
