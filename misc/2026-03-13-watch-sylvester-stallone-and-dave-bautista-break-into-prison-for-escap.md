# Watch Sylvester Stallone and Dave Bautista Break Into Prison For 'Escape Plan 2'

As long as Sylvester Stallone is going to remain Rambo-ripped well into his 70's, the man might as well keep making the biggest, most badass action movies possible.

The latest: Escape Plan 2: Hades, the follow-up to the 2013 prison break blockbuster that co-starred Stallone's longtime rival in jacked-ness, Arnold Schwarzenegger.

Arnie is out for the sequel, but his massive presence will be replaced by the similarly large Dave Bautista — a.k.a Drax the Destroyer from the Marvel Cinematic Universe — who plays an old buddy of Stallone's Ray Breslin. The above-average-sized duo are forced to break into the most high-tech, securest jail on Earth after a member of Breslin's elite security team is wrongly imprisoned. Many people will be punched.
