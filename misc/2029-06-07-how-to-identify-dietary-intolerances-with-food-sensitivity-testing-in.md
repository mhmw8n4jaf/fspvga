What you need to know

Common food sensitivities can manifest as unpleasant symptoms, from headaches and joint pain to gas and bloating. To pinpoint your food intolerances, you’ll want to complete an elimination diet or a food sensitivity test. A food sensitivity test may be right for you if you are experiencing non-specific symptoms like fatigue or moodiness without a clear cause.

In-home lab tests are convenient, affordable, and provide a more accurate measurement of your immune system response than blood tests. Once you’ve identified problem foods, eliminate them from your diet to prevent negative reactions.

Benefits of in-home tests

Being able to carry out food sensitivity testing at home is a major plus, as you don’t have to wait weeks for test results, and you can get started on treatment right away. In-home tests are more convenient than going into a clinic for blood or skin prick tests.
