# Celebrating Fit, Form, and Function

D ENIM IS DEMOCRACY, cut and stitched. It’s a fabric that belongs to everyone—no matter who you are or what you do. You’ll see it on the guy clocking miles on his bike before breakfast, the trainer tossing on a jacket after the gym, the creative director grabbing an espresso in a listening room, and the dad hustling between soccer games. The appeal is its versatility: dark and sharp one moment, washed and easy the next. Denim adapts, shapeshifts, and keeps showing up.

Few brands understand that better than Tommy Hilfiger. Since launching in 1985, the brand has built an empire on the tenent that style should be both classic and personal. Denim has been the core of this philosophy, cut for different walks of life and shaped to suit any lifestyle without losing an ounce of style. It’s as much about self-expression as it is about construction. And for fall 2025, it is doubling down, refining the formula while celebrating 40 years of cultural resonance.
